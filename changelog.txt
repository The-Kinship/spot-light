0.4.0: Notes: write why an item is important (e.g. "for the stuffed peppers recipe") in the Spot Light window, or right-click an important item > "Spot Light: add a note". The note shows in a clear box above the game's tooltip when you mouse over the item (adjustable text size). Saddlebag: reads the window more ways so highlights can find the right bag. New "/spotlight debug" prints what Spot Light sees in open storage windows.
0.3.0: Highlights everywhere: the normal and large inventory layouts, saddlebag (including premium), retainer bags and the FC chest. Tabbed windows are matched to the page you're looking at; if it can't tell for sure, it draws nothing rather than the wrong slot.
0.2.0: Highlights! Important items get a thick, bright border and a corner flag in your inventory (the "all bags open" layout for now). Highlight style settings: color (with quick high-contrast picks), border thickness, corner flag, pulse, and darken everything else. Highlights hide under item tooltips and right-click menus so those stay readable.
